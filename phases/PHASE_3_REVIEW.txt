1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 3 establishes the operational management, reporting, and inter-tier communication
layer for SupportFlow with strict, dedicated role segregation across all four personas:

1. Public Customer (Unauthenticated / External):
   - Scope: Self-service knowledge base, ticket submission, and status tracking.
   - Access: Public (`/`, `/support`, `/track/:token`).
   - Clean Separation: Customer-facing submission links (*Submit Ticket*, *Help Center*)
     are strictly hidden from internal staff workflows to prevent operational clutter.

2. Support Agent Dashboard (`/agent/dashboard`):
   - Scope: Individual contributor queue, ticket triage, and supervisor consultations.
   - Access: Strictly reserved for users with role 'agent'.
   - Capabilities: Personal open/in-progress tickets, urgent/overdue triage, filterable
     inbox with pagination, activity history, notifications preview, and a dedicated
     "Team Lead Consultations" hub (`?tab=queries`) to seek guidance on specific tickets.
     Direct "Consult Team Lead" action is also accessible inside ticket details (`/agent/tickets/:id`).

3. Team Lead Portal (`/lead/dashboard`):
   - Scope: Dedicated team leadership, queue triage, agent mentoring, and executive escalation.
   - Access: Strictly reserved for users with role 'lead'.
   - Capabilities:
     a) Team Workload Analytics: Agent capacity comparisons, unassigned & overdue queues,
        and instant reassignment controls (`ReassignDropdown`).
     b) Agent Inquiries & Guidance Console (`?tab=queries`): Review ticket queries opened by
        agents, reply with technical/policy guidance, and resolve inquiries.
     c) Admin Reports & Escalations Hub (`?tab=admin_reports`): Formally report systemic
        bottlenecks, infrastructure outages, or staffing deficits directly to Workspace Admins.

4. Workspace Administrator Portal (`/admin/dashboard`):
   - Scope: Workspace governance, team member provisioning, global queue management,
     settings, immutable audit logs, and operational escalations.
   - Access: Strictly reserved for users with role 'admin'.
   - Capabilities: Real-time workspace overview KPIs, complete staff roster management
     (add user, change role, toggle active status), global reassignment queue, SLA/workspace
     settings configuration, immutable audit ledger, and a dedicated "Team Lead Reports &
     Escalations" console (`?tab=reports`) to review incident reports, issue binding executive
     directives, and resolve operational escalations.

All dashboard statistics, workload metrics, consultation threads, operational reports, and
audit logs are driven by real backend data computed via MongoDB queries and Express controllers.
Strict backend RBAC authorization ensures that frontend visibility guards are always backed
by HTTP 403 Forbidden enforcement on the API server.

All Phase 3 requirements and post-review enhancements have been built and verified with:
- 43/43 passing automated integration tests across 4 test suites (Vitest).
- 100% clean TypeScript compilation (0 errors across backend and frontend).
- Production bundle verification via Vite (`npm run build` succeeds cleanly).

3. DATA FLOWS & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Agent Dashboard & Ticket Consultation Flow:
   1. Frontend (`/agent/dashboard`): Dispatches TanStack Query `['agentSummary']` to `GET /api/reports/agent-summary`.
   2. Express Auth Middleware: Authenticates JWT token and verifies `role === 'agent'`.
   3. Service Layer (`getAgentDashboardSummary`):
      - Calculates real-time counts for tickets assigned to `req.user._id` (`open`, `in_progress`, `highPriority`, `overdue`).
      - Identifies overdue items based on SLA thresholds (P1 > 4h, P2 > 24h, P3 > 48h, P4 > 72h).
      - Retrieves the agent's recent activity stream from `AuditEvent`.
   4. UI View: Displays real StatCards, Notifications banner, "My Active Tickets" tab, "Urgent & Overdue Work" tab,
      and "Full Ticket Inbox" tab with server-side filters and pagination.
   5. Agent-to-Lead Ticket Consultation Workflow:
      - Directly within the Ticket Detail view (`/agent/tickets/:id`) or the "Lead Consultations" tab (`?tab=queries`),
        the agent clicks "Consult Team Lead".
      - Submits inquiry with `ticketId`, `subject`, `category` (`technical_blocker`, `policy_exception`, `customer_dispute`, etc.),
        and `urgency` (`low`, `normal`, `high`, `urgent`).
      - Calls `POST /api/ticket-queries`. Creates MongoDB `TicketQuery` document, starts a threaded conversation,
        and logs an audit event.
      - Team Lead replies with guidance; the agent receives updates and can continue the conversation or mark it resolved.

B. Team Lead Dashboard & Operational Escalation Flow:
   1. Frontend (`/lead/dashboard`): Guarded by `<RoleRoute allowedRoles={['lead']}>`. Offers 3 major sections:
      - Section 1: "Workload & Queue Triage" (`tab=workload`): Dispatches query `['teamLeadReport']` to `GET /api/reports/team`.
        Displays team workload, live agent capacity comparison, unassigned queue, and overdue triage with `ReassignDropdown`.
      - Section 2: "Agent Inquiries & Guidance" (`tab=queries`): Dispatches query `['ticketQueriesList']` to `GET /api/ticket-queries`.
        Allows Team Lead to filter inquiries, inspect ticket context, provide guidance replies via `POST /api/ticket-queries/:id/messages`,
        or resolve the query via `PATCH /api/ticket-queries/:id/status`.
      - Section 3: "Admin Reports & Escalations" (`tab=admin_reports`): Dispatches query `['adminReportsList']` to `GET /api/admin-reports`.
        Allows Team Lead to submit formal operational reports to Admin regarding systemic issues (e.g. database pool exhaustion,
        shift deficits) via modal calling `POST /api/admin-reports`. Tracks official executive directives issued by Admin.

C. Workspace Administrator Dashboard Flow:
   1. Frontend (`/admin/dashboard`): Guarded by `<RoleRoute allowedRoles={['admin']}>`.
   2. Workspace Overview Tab (`tab=overview`): Real-time metrics computed dynamically from MongoDB.
   3. Team Lead Reports & Escalations Hub (`tab=reports`):
      - Surfaces all operational reports and incident escalations submitted by Team Leads.
      - Stat cards highlight total escalations, critical items, pending review, and resolved reports.
      - Filterable by Status and Priority.
      - "Issue Directive & Resolve" modal: Admin reviews findings, enters official executive directives,
        and transitions report status (`acknowledged`, `in_progress`, `resolved`, `closed`) via `PATCH /api/admin-reports/:id/status`.
   4. Staff Roster Management (`tab=team`):
      - Lists roster with role badges and active indicators.
      - "Add Team Member" modal invokes `POST /api/users` with bcrypt password hashing and email uniqueness validation.
      - Role modifications and deactivations invoke `PATCH /api/users/:id`. Safety guard prevents demoting the last active administrator.
   5. Global Reassignments (`tab=reassign`): Reallocate tickets across agents.
   6. Workspace Settings (`tab=settings`): Update workspace name, default SLA priority, and customer reopen toggles.
   7. Immutable Audit Ledger (`tab=audit`): Paginated audit stream of all workspace actions.

5. POST-REVIEW MODIFICATIONS SUMMARY (PHASE 3 ENHANCEMENTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Customer vs Staff Navbar Sanitization:
   - Problem Identified: Agent dashboard previously contained "Submit ticket" and "Help center"
     links which are customer-facing and cluttered the staff workspace.
   - Solution Implemented: Modified `Navbar.tsx` so that public actions are only visible to
     unauthenticated visitors (`!user`). Staff members see strictly operational links corresponding
     to their role, plus an unobtrusive external preview link to view the knowledge base.

2. Agent <-> Team Lead Ticket Queries (`TicketQuery`):
   - Problem Identified: Agents resolving tickets needed a structured, internal communication channel
     to ask Team Leads questions tied to specific tickets without exposing chatter to customers.
   - Solution Implemented:
     - Created `TicketQuery` model with fields for `ticketId`, `agentId`, `teamLeadId`, `subject`,
       `category`, `urgency`, and threaded `messages`.
     - Added endpoints at `/api/ticket-queries` with strict RBAC (Agent creates, Lead/Admin responds).
     - Integrated quick-action button on `TicketDetailPage.tsx` and dedicated tab on `AgentDashboardPage.tsx`.
     - Integrated supervisor guidance console on `TeamLeadDashboardPage.tsx` with one-click resolution.

3. Team Lead <-> Admin Operational Escalations (`AdminReport`):
   - Problem Identified: Team Leads lacked a formal reporting channel to escalate systemic bottlenecks,
     infrastructure outages, staffing deficits, or policy inquiries to Workspace Admins.
   - Solution Implemented:
     - Created `AdminReport` model with fields for `leadId`, `title`, `category`, `priority`, `summary`,
       `actionRequired`, optional `ticketId`, threaded `messages`, and Admin's binding `adminDirective`.
     - Added endpoints at `/api/admin-reports` with strict RBAC (Lead creates, Admin issues directives).
     - Integrated report submission modal and status tracker on `TeamLeadDashboardPage.tsx`.
     - Integrated executive directive & resolution console on `AdminDashboardPage.tsx`.

4. Comprehensive 4-Role Persona Alignment:
   - Re-evaluated the workflow from the perspective of all 4 personas:
     - Customer: Self-service & track. Never sees internal chatter or staff navigation.
     - Agent: Queue execution, SLA urgency, and supervisor consultation.
     - Team Lead: Workload rebalancing, agent guidance, and administrative escalation.
     - Admin: Global tenant governance, user management, audit trails, and executive directives.
